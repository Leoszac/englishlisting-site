Free online resources to learn English

62 websites, apps, podcasts and tools, reviewed in our own words. "Free tier" means the free part is genuinely useful but some features cost money. Every link checked on November 13, 2026.

62 resources

  • Freepodcast
    6 Minute English (BBC)

    Two presenters discuss one topic in about six minutes, teaching a handful of useful words along the way. Transcript and vocabulary list for every episode.

    intermediate · advanced · British

  • Freepodcast
    All Ears English Podcast

    Long-running podcast where American hosts chat about natural expressions, small talk and culture. Episodes are free; transcripts and courses are paid.

    intermediate · advanced · American

  • Freewebsite
    American English (U.S. Department of State)

    Free materials for learners and teachers of American English, including audio series, games and teaching resources.

    beginner · intermediate · advanced · American

  • Free tierapp
    Anki

    Spaced-repetition flashcards. Free on desktop, web and Android; the official iPhone app is paid.

    beginner · intermediate · advanced · Any

  • Freetool
    Audacity

    Free, open-source audio recorder and editor for Windows, Mac and Linux. Slow down native audio without changing pitch and record yourself on a second track.

    intermediate · advanced · Any

  • Freewebsite
    BBC Learning English

    Structured courses from beginner to advanced plus short recurring series (6 Minute English, The English We Speak, pronunciation videos). Every audio has a transcript.

    beginner · intermediate · advanced · British

  • Freewebsite
    Breaking News English

    Current news stories at several levels with listening, dictation, discussion questions and printable worksheets. Popular with teachers.

    beginner · intermediate · advanced · British

  • FreetoolSpanish support
    Cambridge Dictionary

    Learner-friendly definitions with separate US and UK audio and IPA for each word, plus an English–Spanish dictionary.

    beginner · intermediate · advanced · American and British

  • Freewebsite
    Cambridge English for learners

    Free level test, practice activities and sample papers for Cambridge exams (B2 First, C1 Advanced, C2 Proficiency).

    beginner · intermediate · advanced · British

  • FreewebsiteSpanish support
    Conversation Exchange

    Simple, free site to find a language partner for face-to-face meetings in your city, video calls or pen-pal writing.

    intermediate · advanced · Many

  • Free tiercourseSpanish support
    Coursera (audit English courses)

    University English courses (business English, academic writing, pronunciation). Many can be audited for free; certificates are paid.

    intermediate · advanced · American and others

  • Freewebsite
    Daily Dictation

    Listen to short audio clips and type exactly what you hear, sentence by sentence, with instant checking.

    beginner · intermediate · advanced · American

  • Free tiertoolSpanish support
    DeepL Translator

    Machine translator with natural-sounding output and alternative phrasings; free with character limits.

    intermediate · advanced · American and British

  • Free tierappSpanish support
    Duolingo

    Gamified app with a free, ad-supported tier; can be studied from Spanish. Good for a daily habit, weak on real conversation and natural pronunciation.

    beginner · American

  • Free tiercourseSpanish support
    edX (audit English courses)

    University courses including English for careers and academic English; many offer a free audit track for a limited time, with paid certificates.

    intermediate · advanced · American and others

  • Freewebsite
    ELLLO

    Thousands of short, unscripted conversations between speakers from dozens of countries, with transcripts and quizzes.

    beginner · intermediate · advanced · Many world accents

  • Free tierappSpanish support
    ELSA Speak

    App that scores your pronunciation of words and sentences using speech recognition. A limited set of lessons is free; most content requires a subscription.

    intermediate · advanced · American

  • Freeyoutube
    English with Lucy (YouTube)

    Popular British English channel covering pronunciation, vocabulary and grammar.

    intermediate · advanced · British

  • Freewebsite
    engVid

    Short video lessons by a group of teachers, each followed by a quiz. Covers grammar, idioms, pronunciation, business English and IELTS/TOEFL.

    beginner · intermediate · advanced · American, British, Canadian

  • Freetool
    Forvo

    Crowdsourced pronunciation dictionary: native speakers record individual words, names and place names, tagged by country.

    beginner · intermediate · advanced · Many (filter by country)

  • FreecourseSpanish support
    GCFGlobal LearnFree

    Free nonprofit tutorials on reading, computer skills, job search and everyday life, with a Spanish-language version (GCFAprendeLibre).

    beginner · intermediate · American

  • Free tiertool
    Grammarly (free plan)

    Browser extension and app that flags grammar, spelling and punctuation errors as you write. The free plan covers basic corrections.

    intermediate · advanced · American

  • Free tierappSpanish support
    HelloTalk

    Language-exchange chat app with built-in correction tools; partners can correct your messages and voice notes. Free tier with paid extras.

    intermediate · advanced · Many

  • Free tiertool
    Hemingway Editor

    Paste your text into the free web editor and it highlights long, hard-to-read sentences and passive voice.

    advanced · American

  • Free tierwebsite
    IELTS preparation resources

    Official IELTS hub linking to free sample tests and preparation material from the test partners.

    intermediate · advanced · British and others

  • Freetool
    International Dialects of English Archive

    Free archive of recordings of English speakers from around the world, originally made for actors learning accents.

    advanced · Regional accents worldwide

  • Freecourse
    Khan Academy Grammar

    Free, nonprofit grammar course (parts of speech, punctuation, syntax) with videos and practice. Written for native speakers, so it is great for advanced learners.

    intermediate · advanced · American

  • Free tiertoolSpanish support
    Language Reactor

    Browser extension that shows two subtitle tracks (English + Spanish) on YouTube and Netflix and lets you replay line by line. Core features are free.

    intermediate · advanced · Any

  • Free tiertoolSpanish support
    LanguageTool

    Open-source grammar and style checker for English and Spanish, with a free browser extension and paid premium.

    intermediate · advanced · American and British

  • Freewebsite
    LearnEnglish (British Council)

    Large library of free grammar explanations, graded reading and listening tasks with exercises, organized by CEFR level (A1–C1).

    beginner · intermediate · advanced · British

  • Freewebsite
    LibriVox

    Free public-domain audiobooks read by volunteers; the original texts are free on Project Gutenberg, so you can read and listen at the same time.

    intermediate · advanced · Mixed

  • Freepodcast
    Luke's English Podcast

    Long, entertaining episodes by a British teacher and comedian, ideal for upper-intermediate and advanced listeners.

    advanced · British

  • Free tierwebsiteSpanish support
    LyricsTraining

    Fill in the missing words of song lyrics while the music video plays. Free with ads.

    beginner · intermediate · advanced · Mixed

  • Free with library cardappSpanish support
    Mango Languages (free with library card)

    Paid language app that many US public libraries offer free to cardholders, including English courses taught from Spanish.

    beginner · intermediate · American

  • Freewebsite
    ManyThings.org

    Old-school but huge collection of quizzes, minimal-pair listening tests, word games and sentence lists for ESL students.

    beginner · intermediate · American

  • Free tierwebsite
    Meetup (English conversation groups)

    Search 'English conversation' or 'language exchange' in your city. Most groups meet free in cafés or libraries; a few charge a small fee.

    intermediate · advanced · Many

  • Freetool
    Merriam-Webster

    The reference American English dictionary, with audio pronunciations and example sentences.

    intermediate · advanced · American

  • Freeyoutube
    mmmEnglish (YouTube)

    Well-produced lessons on pronunciation, speaking confidence and common mistakes; includes many imitation exercises.

    intermediate · advanced · Australian

  • FreewebsiteSpanish support
    National Literacy Directory

    Searchable national directory of adult education and English (ESL) programs in the US. Search by ZIP code; there is also a free helpline.

    beginner · intermediate · advanced · American

  • Freewebsite
    News in Levels

    Each news story is rewritten at three difficulty levels, with audio.

    beginner · intermediate · Mixed

  • Freetool
    Oxford Learner's Dictionaries

    Dictionary written for learners, with US and UK audio, collocations and word-frequency labels (the Oxford 3000/5000 lists).

    beginner · intermediate · advanced · American and British

  • Freetool
    Praat

    Free phonetics software used by researchers. Record yourself and see your pitch (intonation) line and vowel quality, then compare with a native recording.

    advanced · Any

  • Freewebsite
    Purdue OWL

    The standard US reference for academic and professional writing: grammar, punctuation, citations, résumés and cover letters, plus an ESL section.

    advanced · American

  • Free tierappSpanish support
    Quizlet

    Flashcard sets made by teachers and students, including many ESL and citizenship-test sets. Basic study modes are free.

    beginner · intermediate · Any

  • Free tieryoutube
    Rachel's English (YouTube)

    One of the largest free libraries of American pronunciation videos: individual sounds, linking, reductions, and real-conversation analysis. A paid academy exists; the YouTube channel is free.

    intermediate · advanced · American

  • Freewebsite
    Randall's ESL Cyber Listening Lab

    Hundreds of everyday-situation listening activities (renting, shopping, job interviews) with comprehension quizzes and scripts, sorted by difficulty.

    beginner · intermediate · advanced · American

  • Freewebsite
    ReadTheory

    Adaptive reading-comprehension practice that adjusts passage difficulty to your answers.

    beginner · intermediate · advanced · American

  • Free tiertoolSpanish support
    Reverso Context

    Shows a word or expression inside real translated sentences (Spanish ↔ English), so you see how it's actually used.

    intermediate · advanced · Mixed

  • Freetool
    Sounds of Speech (University of Iowa)

    Animated diagrams and videos showing how the mouth, tongue and lips form each sound of American English.

    beginner · intermediate · advanced · American

  • Freetool
    Speak & Improve (Cambridge)

    Speak answers to questions and an automatic system estimates your level and gives feedback. Built by Cambridge researchers; free to use.

    intermediate · advanced · British

  • Free tierpodcast
    Speak English Now Podcast

    Podcast focused on speaking fluently and learning without memorizing grammar rules; free episodes with paid extras.

    intermediate · American

  • Freeyoutube
    Speak English with Vanessa (YouTube)

    Friendly American teacher focused on natural, everyday spoken English: phrasal verbs, fast speech and conversation.

    intermediate · advanced · American

  • FreetoolSpanish support
    Speech Accent Archive (George Mason University)

    Thousands of people from different language backgrounds read the same English paragraph, with phonetic transcriptions. Includes many native Spanish speakers and American English speakers.

    advanced · Hundreds of accents

  • Free tierapp
    Speechling

    Record yourself saying sentences and get feedback from human coaches. The free plan includes a limited number of corrections per month.

    intermediate · advanced · American and British

  • Free tierappSpanish support
    Tandem

    Language-exchange app: you help a native English speaker with Spanish and they help you with English, by text, voice or video. Core exchange is free.

    intermediate · advanced · Many

  • FreewebsiteSpanish support
    TED Talks

    Thousands of talks with interactive transcripts and subtitles in many languages, including Spanish.

    advanced · Many

  • Freepodcast
    The InFluency Podcast (Hadar Shemesh)

    Podcast by a non-native American-accent coach about pronunciation, confidence and sounding clear as a non-native speaker.

    advanced · American

  • Free tierwebsite
    TOEFL free practice (ETS)

    Official TOEFL iBT preparation page from the test maker, including free practice tests and sample questions alongside paid prep.

    intermediate · advanced · American

  • FreecourseSpanish support
    USA Learns

    Complete video-based English courses for adults, built around everyday life in the US (work, doctor, school). Instructions are available in Spanish. No cost and no ads.

    beginner · intermediate · American

  • Freewebsite
    VOA Learning English

    News stories read more slowly and with simpler vocabulary, with the full text next to the audio. American English.

    beginner · intermediate · American

  • FreetoolSpanish support
    WordReference

    English–Spanish dictionary with very active forums where translators debate exact nuances of words and expressions.

    beginner · intermediate · advanced · American and British

  • Freetool
    YouGlish

    Type any word or phrase and hear it said by real people in thousands of YouTube clips, filtered by US, UK or Australian English.

    intermediate · advanced · American, British and more
