Other free English classes in the New York City area

  • Free Public adult school in-person
    NYC Public Schools Adult Education (ESOL)

    New York, NY

    The city's public adult education system runs English classes for New Yorkers 21+ at sites in all five boroughs, mornings, afternoons and evenings.

    Free for NYC residents 21 and older; no Social Security number needed for ESOL.

  • Free Government program in-persononline
    We Speak NYC conversation classes

    New York, NY

    City-run English conversation classes built around videos about life in NYC; held online and at libraries and community spaces.

    Free weekly conversation classes for beginner and intermediate learners.

  • Free Public library in-persononline
    Queens Public Library ESOL (New Americans Program)

    Queens, NY

    Beginner to advanced English classes, conversation practice groups and English-plus-computer-skills classes run by the library's New Americans Program.

    Free; conversation groups need no registration.
