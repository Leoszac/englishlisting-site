Frequently asked questions

Is EnglishListing free?+

Yes. Everything on EnglishListing is free to use, and nobody pays to be listed or ranked. We don't use affiliate links.

Where can I find free English classes near me?+

Start with our directory of free ESL classes in the US (102 verified programs so far). If your city isn't listed yet, search the National Literacy Directory by ZIP code, call or text 211, or ask your public library.

What is the best free way to learn English online?+

It depends on your level. Beginners do well with full free courses like USA Learns or BBC Learning English. Intermediate and advanced learners should combine daily listening with transcripts, conversation practice and pronunciation work. We review 62 free resources, 40 of them completely free.

How do I verify a free English class is legitimate?+

Every listing on EnglishListing links to the provider's official page and shows the public source we used to confirm the cost, plus the verification date. Public libraries, school districts, community colleges and established nonprofits are the most reliable providers.

My English is good, so why do I still sound foreign?+

For most advanced speakers the cause is prosody: word stress, sentence rhythm, reduced function words and intonation, rather than grammar or vocabulary. Our free accent training guides cover each piece in order.

Do I need documents to take free ESL classes?+

Requirements vary. Many library and nonprofit programs ask for little or nothing; some public programs ask for ID, proof of address or a minimum age; a few workforce-funded programs ask for work authorization. Each listing shows the requirement when the provider publishes it.
