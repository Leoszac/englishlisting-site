Free ESL classes in Nevada

3 English programs for adults verified in Nevada so far (3 with no tuition at all). Checked against official sources on November 13, 2026.

Las Vegas (3)

Las Vegas area

  • Free Public library in-person
    Las Vegas-Clark County Library District Adult Learning (ESL)

    Las Vegas, NV

    No-cost English classes at all levels at several library branches, with placement testing.

    No cost; 18+; photo ID for the placement test.

  • Free Public adult school in-persononline
    CCSD Family Support Centers English classes

    Las Vegas, NV

    Free in-person and virtual English classes for families of Clark County School District students, plus a free Rosetta Stone license.

    Free; for CCSD parents and caregivers.

  • Free Nonprofit online
    Catholic Charities of Southern Nevada English Language Program

    Las Vegas, NV

    Free online beginning, intermediate and advanced English classes for anyone 16+ not enrolled in high school.

    Free to the public.

Frequently asked questions

Are there free English classes in Nevada?+

Yes. We have verified 3 English programs for adults in Nevada so far, 3 of them with no tuition, at public libraries, adult schools, community colleges and nonprofits.

Can I take free ESL classes online in Nevada?+

2 of the Nevada programs we list offer online or hybrid classes. Free national online courses like USA Learns are also available anywhere.

How do I find a free English class in Nevada outside the cities listed?+

Search the National Literacy Directory by ZIP code, call or text 211, ask at your public library, or look for "adult education" or "noncredit ESL" at your local school district or community college.

What does "free if eligible" mean?+

The program is tuition-free for people who meet its conditions, typically age (16, 18 or 21+), residency in the city, county or state, or showing an ID. Most adults qualify, but check before you go.

Elsewhere in Nevada?

Search the National Literacy Directory by ZIP code, dial 211, or ask your public library. Read our guide to finding free classes. While you wait for a spot, free online resources help you keep going.
