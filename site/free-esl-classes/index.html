Your city isn't listed yet?

We add cities every week. Meanwhile, these free tools cover the whole country:

  • National Literacy Directory: search adult ESL programs by ZIP code.
  • Call or text 211: a free local helpline that can point you to English classes.
  • Ask at your public library: many host classes or conversation groups, and a free card often unlocks apps like Mango Languages.
  • Search your local community college for "noncredit ESL" or "adult education" (not "academic ESL", which usually costs money).

What "free" means here

  • Free No tuition. You may still pay small costs like a textbook; we note them when the source does.
  • Free if eligible Free for people who meet conditions (age, residency, ID). Usually most adults qualify.
  • Low cost A small fee, typically under $100 per term. Listed when free options are scarce.
Independent listing: EnglishListing is not affiliated with the programs and resources it lists, and no one pays to be listed. Class schedules, eligibility and costs change: always confirm with the provider before you go. Spotted something out of date? Tell us.
