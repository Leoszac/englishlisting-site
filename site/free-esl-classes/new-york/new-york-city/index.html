Free ESL classes in New York City

5 English programs for adults in the New York City area, all with no tuition. Each one checked against an official source on November 13, 2026.

Public libraries

  • Free Public library in-persononline
    Brooklyn Public Library English classes

    Brooklyn, NY

    Free English classes, conversation groups and business English at branches across Brooklyn, including walk-in We Speak NYC sessions.

    Free; some classes need no registration.

  • Free Public library in-persononline
    Queens Public Library ESOL (New Americans Program)

    Queens, NY

    Beginner to advanced English classes, conversation practice groups and English-plus-computer-skills classes run by the library's New Americans Program.

    Free; conversation groups need no registration.

  • Free Public library in-persononline
    New York Public Library English classes

    Manhattan, NY

    Multi-week English courses at several levels plus We Speak NYC conversation classes at NYPL branches and online.

    Free; for adults who live, work or study in NYC (Manhattan, Bronx, Staten Island branches).

Public adult schools

  • Free Public adult school in-person
    NYC Public Schools Adult Education (ESOL)

    New York, NY

    The city's public adult education system runs English classes for New Yorkers 21+ at sites in all five boroughs, mornings, afternoons and evenings.

    Free for NYC residents 21 and older; no Social Security number needed for ESOL.

Government programs

  • Free Government program in-persononline
    We Speak NYC conversation classes

    New York, NY

    City-run English conversation classes built around videos about life in NYC; held online and at libraries and community spaces.

    Free weekly conversation classes for beginner and intermediate learners.

Free English classes in New York City: FAQ

Are there free English classes in New York City?+

Yes. We verified 5 English programs for adults in the New York City area, 5 of them with no tuition. Providers include public libraries, public adult schools, government programs.

Can I take free ESL classes online in New York City?+

4 of the programs listed offer online or hybrid options, including We Speak NYC conversation classes, Brooklyn Public Library English classes, Queens Public Library ESOL (New Americans Program).

What kinds of places offer free English classes in New York City?+

In New York City, free English classes are offered by public libraries, public adult schools, government programs. Libraries and nonprofits are often the easiest to join; public adult schools and community colleges usually offer the most structured levels.

Do I need documents to enroll?+

It depends on the program. Some ask for ID or proof of address, a few require work authorization, and others require nothing. Each listing shows the requirement when the provider publishes it; confirm with the provider before you go.

What if all the free classes are full?+

Join more than one waitlist, ask about open-entry classes that let you start mid-term, look for library conversation groups that need no registration, and keep practicing with free online courses while you wait.

Tips before you sign up

  • Popular free classes fill up. Get on more than one waitlist and keep practicing online meanwhile (free online resources).
  • Most programs start with a short placement test so you land in the right level.
  • Already advanced? Classes may feel too easy. Conversation groups and accent training are usually a better fit.
